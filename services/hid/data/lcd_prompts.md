# LCD source prompts

These text prompts describe the generated source art referenced by
`lcd_assets.json`. The PNG sources are copied into ignored
`.local-assets/lcd/` and are never committed.

## Focus Core

Square abstract dark-tech icon for a tiny keyboard LCD. Deep black and navy
background. A precise cyan electric ring around a faceted luminous blue core,
with four restrained violet star points. Centered, symmetrical, high contrast,
no text, no logo, no border, no fine detail that disappears at 128x128.

## Aurora

Square abstract keyboard LCD wallpaper. Black and midnight-blue background.
Wide translucent cyan light ribbons sweep from the upper left and cross smooth
violet ribbons from the upper right, converging at a bright central point.
Minimal composition, high contrast, soft glow, no text, no logo, no border,
legible at 128x128.
